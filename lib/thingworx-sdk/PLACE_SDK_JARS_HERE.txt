<ThingWorx-install>/ThingWorx/WEB-INF/lib/

Required JARs:
- Thingworx.Foundation-*.jar
- log4j-*.jar
- slf4j-api-*.jar

They are required before Task 6 (FTPConnectionThing) can be compiled.
